THE STATUE THAT WAS ALWAYS THERE.

No inscription. No record of delivery. Beside the White House, everyone walks past it as though it has always belonged.

Face the boss to guard. Attacks spend stamina. Dodge or retreat during the long wind-up; strike during recovery. Demo wealth buttons simulate game progression. Progression thresholds are editable.

Prototype combat and generated key poses. Plain JavaScript model, atlas coordinates, hitboxes, timings and a Godot import helper are supplied for integration.
